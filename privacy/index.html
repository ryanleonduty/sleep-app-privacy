Privacy policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Product-name note: The application is currently under development and may be released under a different public name. This policy will be updated before public release if the name or data practices change.

This Privacy Policy explains how Ryan Le handles information in the sleep companion application currently under development (the “App”). The App is designed to help users understand sleep and naps while keeping data local-first and avoiding account-based tracking.

Information the App can access

  • Health and sleep data. If you grant permission, the App reads sleep and related wellness data from Apple HealthKit to build a private sleep timeline. The App uses HealthKit as a read-only source in the current product design and does not write app-owned sessions back to HealthKit.
  • App-created sleep data. This includes app-owned sleep or nap sessions, manual entries, corrections, preferences, and data needed to provide exports and deletion controls.
  • Optional sleep-sound features. If you enable an available sleep-sound feature, the iPhone microphone may record short sound snippets for the feature. Processing is intended to occur on the device; the snippets are not sent to Ryan Le for advertising or profiling.
  • Purchase information. Subscriptions and transactions are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The App does not receive your payment-card number.

How information is used

Information is used to display sleep timelines, summaries, nap timing, trends, exports, user-controlled corrections, and other features that you request. The App does not sell sleep or health information and does not use it for advertising.

Where information is stored

  • On your devices. The App is local-first. App-created data is stored on the iPhone or Apple Watch as applicable.
  • Private iCloud storage. If an optional private iCloud synchronization feature is enabled and available, eligible app-created data may be stored in your private iCloud container under your Apple account. It is not a public database operated by Ryan Le.
  • Apple services. HealthKit and App Store transactions remain subject to Apple’s own terms and privacy practices.

Sharing

Ryan Le does not sell your information. The App does not require an account or share your sleep data with advertisers. Information may be handled by Apple services when required for HealthKit, private iCloud storage, or App Store transactions, according to Apple’s policies.

Your controls

You can control HealthKit permissions in Apple Health and iOS Settings. The App provides export and deletion controls for app-created data where the feature is available. Deleting data from the App does not delete the original HealthKit records managed by Apple Health.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. It is intended for general adult consumer use and is not a medical device or medical advice service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App’s name, features, or data practices change. The effective date above will change when a new version is published.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact Ryan Le through the developer profile.
