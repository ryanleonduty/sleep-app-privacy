Privacy policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ryan Le handles information in Mamepi Sleep for iPhone and Apple Watch (the “App”). The App has no account and Ryan Le does not operate a server or cloud-sync service for your sleep data.

Information the App can access

  • Health and sleep data. If you grant permission, the iPhone app reads sleep and related wellness data from Apple HealthKit, including sleep analysis, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, and sleeping wrist temperature when available. HealthKit access is read-only; the App does not write app-owned sessions to Apple Health.
  • App-created sleep data. This includes app-owned sleep and nap sessions, manual entries, corrections, schedules, preferences, and information needed for exports and deletion controls. This data is stored on your devices and may be exchanged between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch to provide the App’s features.
  • Apple Watch motion activity. If available, the Watch app reads motion-activity history and reduces it to minute-level still or active summaries for sleep estimates. Those summaries are sent only to your paired iPhone for processing; the App does not send them to Ryan Le or an app-operated server.
  • Purchases. Apple handles subscriptions and transactions through the App Store and StoreKit. The App reads the product and entitlement status needed to provide purchased features, but does not receive your payment-card number.

How information is used

Information is used to display sleep timelines, summaries, nap timing, trends, user-controlled corrections, and other features you request. The App does not sell sleep or health information or use it for advertising.

Where information is stored

  • On your devices. App-created data is stored on your iPhone or Apple Watch as applicable. Tracking data may be exchanged between your paired devices.
  • No App-operated cloud service. The App does not upload sleep data to a server or provide iCloud synchronization. Apple’s or your device’s backup behavior, if enabled, is governed by Apple and your device settings and is outside the App’s control.
  • Apple services. HealthKit and App Store transactions remain subject to Apple’s own terms and privacy practices.

Sharing

Ryan Le does not receive your sleep or health data and does not sell it or share it with advertisers. When you explicitly export data, you choose the destination; that destination may handle the export under its own privacy practices. Apple handles HealthKit and App Store transactions under its policies.

Your controls

You can control HealthKit permissions in Apple Health and iOS Settings. You can export or erase the App’s data in Settings → Privacy & data. Deleting data from the App does not delete the original HealthKit records managed by Apple Health.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. It is intended for general adult consumer use and is not a medical device or medical advice service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App’s name, features, or data practices change. The effective date above will change when a new version is published.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, visit the Mamepi Sleep support page.
