Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ryan Le handles information in Mamepi Sleep for iPhone and Apple Watch (the “App”). The App helps you understand your sleep and naps. It has no account and no server: your sleep data stays on your devices.

Information the App can access

  • Health and sleep data. If you grant permission, the App reads sleep and related wellness data from Apple HealthKit to build a private sleep timeline. The App only reads from HealthKit and never writes to it.
  • App-created sleep data. This includes app-owned sleep or nap sessions, manual entries, corrections, preferences, and data needed to provide exports and deletion controls.
  • Apple Watch motion and heart rate. If you allow it, the Apple Watch app uses motion data and heart rate to estimate when you slept. This is processed on your Watch and iPhone and is sent only between your paired Watch and iPhone.
  • Purchase information. Subscriptions and transactions are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The App does not receive your payment-card number.

How information is used

Information is used to display sleep timelines, summaries, nap timing, trends, exports, user-controlled corrections, and other features that you request. The App does not sell sleep or health information and does not use it for advertising.

Where information is stored

  • On your devices. App-created data is stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch. The App does not upload it to any server or to iCloud.
  • Apple services. HealthKit and App Store transactions remain subject to Apple’s own terms and privacy practices.

Sharing

Ryan Le does not sell your information. The App does not require an account or share your sleep data with advertisers. Ryan Le never receives your sleep or health data. Apple handles HealthKit and App Store transactions under its own policies.

Your controls

You can control HealthKit permissions in Apple Health and iOS Settings. You can export or erase the App’s data at any time in Settings → Privacy & data. Deleting data from the App does not delete the original HealthKit records managed by Apple Health.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. It is intended for general adult consumer use and is not a medical device or medical advice service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App’s name, features, or data practices change. The effective date above will change when a new version is published.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact Ryan Le through the developer profile.
